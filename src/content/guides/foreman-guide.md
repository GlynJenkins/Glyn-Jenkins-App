# Foreman Guide — Glyn Jenkins Workforce Portal

A quick, practical guide to using the app as a **Foreman**. Your main job in the app is **booking in your gang's wages every fortnight**. It's designed for your phone.

---

## 1. Getting set up

1. Open the link to the portal in your phone's browser and tap **Admin / Foreman Login**.
2. Sign in with your email and the password you set when you registered.
3. **Add it to your home screen** so it opens like an app: in your browser menu tap **"Add to Home Screen"**. After that, just tap the icon each time.

If you can't get in, your account may not be activated yet — the office activates new logins. Give them a shout.

---

## 2. Your home screen

When you log in you'll see:

- **Claim window** at the top — the current fortnight's **work dates**, the **apply-by** deadline (with a countdown), and the **pay date**. You can only submit a claim while this window is open.
- **Build Fortnightly Claim** — the button to start booking in wages.
- **Past claims** — tap to see previous fortnights and their status.
- **My Sites** — only the sites you're assigned to. Each has **Site Price Grid**, **Submit Variation**, and **Roof Firesocks**.

---

## 3. Booking in your gang's wages (the main job)

Do this once per fortnight, before the apply-by deadline.

1. Tap **Build Fortnightly Claim**.
2. Under **Site Lifts**, tap **Add Lifts** next to a site. This opens that site's price grid.
3. Tap **Select Lifts to Claim**, then tap the lifts your gang has completed:
   - Tap a cell and choose **25% / 50% / 75% / All** of that lift's value.
   - Or tap the **plot number** to take the whole row at once.
   - **Colours tell you the state:** white = not claimed, **orange** = part claimed, **blue** = submitted (waiting for approval), **green** = approved. **Green cells are locked** — they've already been claimed and paid, so you can't book them again.
4. When you've selected everything, tap **Build Claim**. (If you work more than one site, go back and **Add Lifts** from the other site too — it all goes into one claim.)
5. **Select Your Gang** — tick the workers who are on this claim (use the search or the role filters), then **Confirm Gang**.
6. **Pay Allocation** — enter how much of the pool each worker gets. You must allocate **every penny**: the bar at the bottom shows **Pool / Allocated / Remaining** and only lets you submit when it's fully allocated (Remaining £0.00).
7. Tap **Submit Claim**. You'll see **"Claim Submitted — sent to admin for approval."**

That's it — the office reviews and approves it, and the workers get paid on the pay date.

---

## 4. Good to know

- **One claim per fortnight.** If you need to change a submitted claim before it's approved, open it from **Past claims** and **withdraw** it, then build it again.
- **Someone else claimed a lift?** If a lift's already been taken, the app won't let you claim it again — you'll get a message to refresh. This stops two gangs being paid for the same work.
- **Always the latest sheet.** Every time you open a site grid it shows the current state, including lifts other foremen have claimed.
- **Apprentices** — you can add their college and holiday days as part of the claim.

---

## 5. Extra works — Submit a Variation

For work outside the normal price grid (extras, day works):

1. On your site, tap **Submit Variation**.
2. Describe the work, add the hours/details, and **attach photos** as evidence.
3. Submit it — the office reviews and approves it, and once approved you can include it in a fortnightly claim.

---

## 6. Roof Firesocks

1. On your site, tap **Roof Firesocks**.
2. Upload the required evidence **photos per plot**.

Note: some plots need firesock photos before their roof lift can be fully claimed — the app will tell you if evidence is missing.

---

## 7. Checking your claims

Tap **Past claims** on your home screen to see each fortnight and whether it's **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected**. If something's rejected, the office will have added a reason.
